AVS Enterprises is a partnership firm located in the Kalyan-Bhiwandi MIDC industrial area of Maharashtra. We supply electrical testing, measuring and protection equipment to the people who build, inspect and maintain India's electrical infrastructure.

Our customers include electrical contractors, panel builders, factories, power utilities, solar developers, inspection agencies, calibration laboratories and technical institutes. They come to us because they need instruments that give dependable readings, and a supplier who understands how those instruments are used on site.
